REAL STADIUM BACKDROPS
======================
Drop a photo of each ballpark here and it becomes that level's background,
replacing the drawn version. Refresh the game to see it. No file = drawn park.

Use JPG, named EXACTLY like this (wide/landscape photos look best):

  truist.jpg    Level 1  - Truist Park (Braves)
  cbp.jpg       Level 2  - Citizens Bank Park (Phillies)
  dodger.jpg    Level 3  - Dodger Stadium (Dodgers)
  trop.jpg      Level 4  - Tropicana Field (Rays)
  fenway.jpg    Level 5  - Fenway Park (Red Sox)
  yankee.jpg    Level 6  - Yankee Stadium (Yankees)

Tip: Google "<stadium> outfield wide" images, save as the names above.
A wide shot showing the outfield wall works great as a runner backdrop.
