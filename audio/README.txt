STADIUM SOUNDS
==============
Drop short MP3 clips here with these EXACT names. Refresh the game and they
play automatically — in ANY version (V1/V2/V3). No file = quiet (or soft synth
in V3). No robot voice.

  playball.mp3   plays at the START of every game ("Play ball!" announcer)
  crowd.mp3      loops as background stadium crowd (keep ~10-30s; it loops)
  hit.mp3        crack of the bat -- plays on every jump
  homerun.mp3    "HOME RUN!" -- plays when you FINISH a level
  out.mp3        "OUT!" / groan -- plays when you strike out (game over)
  cheer.mp3      crowd cheer (used if homerun.mp3 is missing)
  win.mp3        victory music -- loops during the World Series celebration

HOW TO GET THEM
  1. Use any "YouTube to MP3" website/app on your clips.
  2. Save the MP3s to your Desktop.
  3. Rename to match the names above (all lowercase).
  4. Drop them in this folder (acuna-runner/audio/) and refresh the game.
     (Or just tell me they're on your Desktop and I'll place + rename them.)

Keep them SHORT so they load fast on phones.
